Questions we are asked most.

How long does a flask last?

Worn daily, about four months. The scent keeps for two years unopened.

Is it safe for sensitive skin?

We list every ingredient on the label. Patch-test on the inner wrist before first use.

Can I return a flask?

Unopened flasks return within thirty days. Empty ones come back for refills.

Do you ship outside India?

Yes, tracked and insured, usually within seven working days.
